About PlacementPredict

An applied machine learning system designed to analyze student academic trajectory, technical competencies, and internship experiences to predict university campus placement outcomes.

🎯 Project Mission

Bridge the gap between raw educational data and actionable career counseling. Identify at-risk candidates early in their degree cycle to recommend targeted remedial training in data structures, algorithms, and internships.

🔬 Methodological Rigor

Built in alignment with the university ML Engineering syllabus across six distinct modules (M1 to M6), from regularized linear decision hyperplanes to gradient-boosted ensembles and telemetry dashboards.

💻 Technology Stack

Python 3.14 • Flask Serving Framework • Scikit-Learn • XGBoost • Pandas • NumPy • Matplotlib • Vanilla Modern CSS.
